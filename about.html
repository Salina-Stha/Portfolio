About Me

Hello! I'm Salina, a QA Engineer passionate about ensuring software quality through comprehensive testing, detailed analysis, and continuous improvement. I enjoy identifying defects, designing effective test cases, and helping teams deliver reliable applications.

What I Do

  • Manual Test Cases

    Design and execute manual test cases to validate application functionality, identify defects, and ensure features meet business and user requirements.

  • Functional & Regression Testing

    Perform functional and regression testing to verify new features work as expected while ensuring existing functionality remains stable after changes.

  • Validate Business Requirements

    Analyze and validate business requirements to ensure they are clear, complete, testable, and accurately reflected in the application's behavior.

  • Perform API Testing

    Test APIs using Postman by validating request and response data, status codes, and endpoint functionality to ensure reliable backend communication.

  • Prepare Bug Reports

    Identify, document, and verify software defects by creating detailed bug reports with clear reproduction steps, expected results, actual results, and retesting fixes after implementation.
